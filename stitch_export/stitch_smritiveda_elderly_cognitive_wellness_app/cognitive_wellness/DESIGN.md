## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a dignified, serene, and exquisitely legible interface tailored for senior cognitive wellness, daily memory anchoring, and neurological vitality. The design movement marries **Minimalism** with **Warm Organic Tactility**—discarding both the clinical sterility of hospital software and the patronizing simplifications often forced onto elder-centric products.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Tenor
- **Dignified & Reassuring:** Prioritizes empowerment, lucidity, and respect. It feels like an heirloom leather-bound journal distilled into a modern wearable-connected digital sanctuary.
- **Subtle Cultural Resonance:** Subtly integrates warmth through earthy terracotta (reminiscent of baked clay and saffron) and tranquil temple moss green, grounding clinical cognitive science in organic, restorative warmth.
- **Calm & Unhurried:** Eliminates cognitive clutter, visual noise, flashing indicators, and aggressive micro-animations to prevent visual fatigue and anxiety.

### Core Visual Principles
- **Generous Whitespace as Cognitive Breathing Room:** Abundant negative space isolates tasks, allowing users to process individual cognitive cues or memories without disorientation.
- **Uncompromised High-Contrast Legibility:** WCAG AAA adherence across all body, instruction, and operational tiers without relying on stark, eye-straining pure blacks or harsh pure whites.
- **Physical Touch Target Certainty:** Every actionable touch point guarantees an expansive spatial buffer (minimum 48–56px interactive footprint), accommodating age-related motor tremors or reduced precision.

## Colors

The palette rejects clinical blue and sterile neon tones in favor of restorative, warm earth pigments balanced against an ivory parchment substrate. This structure ensures zero glare, reduced blue-light strain, and visual warmth for aging eyes.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#B85028` - Deep Terracotta):** Represents vitality, warmth, and attention. Applied to primary CTAs, critical cognitive markers, memory completion states, and active stepper indicators.
- **Secondary (`#3D6B58` - Restorative Moss / Sage):** Embodies tranquility, mental balance, and routine consistency. Used for supportive wellness feedback, streak confirmations, health status metrics, and restorative breath exercises.
- **Tertiary (`#8A643E` - Warm Sandalwood):** Handles contextual tags, category chips, archival memories, and subtle thematic accents.
- **Neutral (`#202422` - Deep Forest Charcoal):** Replaces pure black to deliver soft, non-glare, high-contrast clarity against warm ivory backgrounds, exceeding AAA contrast ratios for body typography.

### Functional Surfaces & Tokens
- **Backgrounds:** The primary app backdrop is `canvas-ivory` (`#FBF9F5`), subtly resting above device frames. Nested view containers shift to `surface-cream` (`#F7F4EE`).
- **Cards & Elevating Surfaces:** Actionable cards sit on `card-white` (`#FFFFFF`) with a precision hairline border in `border-subtle` (`#EAE6DF`) to define clear physical boundaries for users with diminished edge contrast sensitivity.
- **Text Hierarchy:**
  - Headlines & Core Data: `text-primary` (`#1F2421`)
  - Informational Labels & Instructions: `text-secondary` (`#4E5450`)
  - Timestamps, Secondary Metadata, Unselected State: `text-muted` (`#6E7571`)

## Typography

The typographic system addresses age-related visual degradation, astigmatism, and contrast sensitivity reduction while retaining literary elegance.

### Font Pairings & Roles
- **Headlines & Editorial Prompts (`Newsreader`):** A literary, humane, and sturdy transitional serif. Its organic serifs provide visual anchors that ease cognitive scanning, framing memory recollections and daily reflections with gravity and cultural depth.
- **Interface, Prompts & Body Copy (`Atkinson Hyperlegible Next`):** Engineered purposefully for low-vision and senior accessibility. Letterforms feature unambiguous distinctions between homoglyphic characters (e.g., uppercase `I`, lowercase `l`, numeral `1`, and zero `0` vs `O`).
- **Line Heights & Text Sizing:** Body sizes start at an accessible minimum of 16px with an exaggerated 1.625x line-height (`26px`) ratio to prevent line skipping or reader disorientation during cognitive exercises.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a relaxed, predictable 8pt spatial grid with generous bounds to mitigate motor accuracy fatigue and prevent cognitive overload.

### Spatial Discipline
- **Interactive Targets:** Critical controls enforce a strict touch target envelope of at least `56px` (`3.5rem`), scaling up to `64px` (`4rem`) for daily action anchors (e.g., "Confirm Medication", "Begin Memory Exercise").
- **Spacing Scale:**
  - `space-xs` (8px) / `space-sm` (12px): Internal chip and badge padding.
  - `space-md` (16px) / `space-lg` (24px): Standard card interior paddings and input field heights.
  - `space-xl` (32px) / `space-2xl` (48px): Section demarcations and cognitive step separation.
  - `space-3xl` (64px): Major task completion landmarks.

### Layout System Across Breakpoints
- **Mobile (<640px):** Single-column fluid view with `1.25rem` screen gutters. Action elements are fixed to bottom sheets or grounded floating bars within comfortable thumb reach.
- **Tablet (641px–1024px):** 6-column grid with `2rem` gutters. Splits context (e.g., daily schedule or memory timeline on the left, active recall exercise card on the right).
- **Desktop (>1025px):** 12-column grid constrained to `72rem` (`1152px`) maximum content width to avoid over-extended reading lengths. Margins auto-center with `2.5rem` outer gutters.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids loud skeuomorphism, aggressive floating drop shadows, or disorienting multi-layered glass blurs. Instead, depth is articulated through **Tonal Layering**, **High-Fidelity Outlines**, and **Soft Warm-Tinted Ambient Shadows**.

### The Layering Stack
1. **Base Layer (Level 0):** `canvas-ivory` (`#FBF9F5`). The primary foundation canvas for the viewport.
2. **Container Tier (Level 1):** `surface-cream` (`#F7F4EE`). Groups related modules, lists, and static schedule views.
3. **Card Tier (Level 2):** `card-white` (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px solid structural border in `border-subtle` (`#EAE6DF`). This level receives an ultra-soft, warm ambient shadow:
   - `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(45, 36, 28, 0.05), 0 1px 4px 0 rgba(45, 36, 28, 0.03);`
4. **Interactive Focus & Floating Prompts (Level 3):** Modal sheets, memory recall carousels, and persistent bottom assistance bars:
   - `box-shadow: 0 12px 36px -4px rgba(45, 36, 28, 0.09), 0 2px 8px -1px rgba(45, 36, 28, 0.04);`
   - Border: 1px solid `#DFD9CE`.

### Contrast Edge Preservation
Shadows never replace structural borders. Elderly vision frequently struggles with luminance-only shadow edges; every elevated surface maintains an explicit low-contrast outline to ensure structural clarity.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a calibrated **Rounded** aesthetic (`roundedness: 2`). This brings welcoming tactile warmth without lapsing into playful, juvenile pill forms that could undermine user dignity.

### Corner Radii Architecture
- **Base Elements (`rounded-base`, 0.5rem / 8px):** Checkboxes, radio indicators, small assistive badges, and inline status tags.
- **Interactive Controls (`rounded-md`, 0.75rem / 12px):** Text input containers, segmented controls, dropdown triggers, and routine list tiles.
- **Cards & Modals (`rounded-lg`, 1rem / 16px):** Primary content cards, medication reminders, memory cards, and dialog windows.
- **Hero Containers (`rounded-xl`, 1.5rem / 24px):** Cognitive exercise overview boards, daily greeting canvases, and wearable vitals overview modules.
- **Pills (`rounded-full`, 9999px):** Audio recording toggles, tactile icon containers, and completion status pills only.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action Button:** 
  - Height: `56px`. Background: `primary_color_hex` (`#B85028`). Text: `#FFFFFF`, `label-lg` (semi-bold Atkinson Hyperlegible, 16px).
  - Shape: `rounded-md` (12px). Border: none. Shadow: `0 2px 6px rgba(184, 80, 40, 0.2)`.
  - Active/Pressed: Darkens to `#9C411E` with a tactile 1px depression transform.
  - Focus Ring: 3px solid `#3D6B58` offset by 2px.
- **Secondary Action Button:**
  - Height: `56px`. Background: `card-white` (`#FFFFFF`). Text: `text-primary` (`#1F2421`).
  - Border: 1.5px solid `border-subtle` (`#EAE6DF`).
  - Hover/Focus State: Background shifts to `surface-cream` (`#F7F4EE`), border shifts to `#B85028`.
- **Gentle Supportive Button:**
  - Height: `48px` to `56px`. Background: `terracotta-soft` (`#F7ECE7`). Text: `primary_color_hex` (`#B85028`). Border: none.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status / Cognitive Category Chips:** Height: `38px` to ensure effortless tap interaction.
- **Default State:** Background `surface-cream` (`#F7F4EE`), 1px border `border-subtle` (`#EAE6DF`), text `text-secondary` (`#4E5450`).
- **Active / Selected State:** Background `sage-soft` (`#EBF2EE`), border `secondary_color_hex` (`#3D6B58`), text `secondary_color_hex` (`#3D6B58`) with an explicit checkmark icon.

### Cards (Memory & Vitality Anchors)
- **Memory Flashcard / Daily Check-in Card:**
  - Surface: `card-white` (`#FFFFFF`), Border: 1px solid `border-subtle` (`#EAE6DF`).
  - Padding: `1.5rem` (`24px`).
  - Layout: Generous vertical stack. Title in `Newsreader` (`headline-sm`, 22px), followed by `body-lg` prompt in `Atkinson Hyperlegible` with 8px margin separation.
  - Audio/Voice Prompt: A dedicated, highly visible speaker button (`48x48px`) in `surface-cream` to trigger slow, warm voice-assisted readout.

### Lists & Activity Rows
- **List Item Container:** Minimum height `72px` to prevent mis-taps.
- **Visual Separation:** Separated by a 1px border (`#EAE6DF`) with `16px` vertical inset padding.
- **Leading Element:** High-contrast `44x44px` soft rounded tile containing recognizable icons (e.g., medicine bottle, walking silhouette, family photograph icon).
- **Trailing Indicator:** Explicit arrow or check button with at least `48px` clickable area.

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- **Container:** Height `58px`. Background: `card-white` (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Border:** 1.5px solid `border-subtle` (`#EAE6DF`). 
- **Focus State:** 2px solid `primary_color_hex` (`#B85028`), without erratic layout shifts.
- **Typography:** Value typed in `body-lg` (18px) `text-primary`. Placeholder text set in `text-muted` (16px) with high readability.
- **Label:** Permanent, explicit label positioned above the input field (never disappearing floating placeholders).

### Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- **Hit Area:** 48x48px wrapper.
- **Control Box:** `26x26px` physical box with `rounded-base` (6px) or circle for radios.
- **Unchecked:** 2px solid `#A8A399` on white.
- **Checked:** Background `primary_color_hex` (`#B85028`) or `secondary_color_hex` (`#3D6B58`) with high-contrast white glyph (stroke width 2.5px).

### Specialty Component: Voice & Memory Assistant Anchor
- A persistently docked or easily accessible floating bar with a 56px circular tactile microphone button in deep terracotta, accompanied by an explicit text label ("Tap to speak or ask Smriti").
